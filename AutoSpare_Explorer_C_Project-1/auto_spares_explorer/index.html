ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Understand the parts.
Understand the ride.

ADN SPARE is an interactive learning project with six vehicle catalogues, an animated exploded view, rotating reference photos of real spare parts, and a personal saved-parts list. Parts and photos are educational references; equipment varies by model year, trim and market.
